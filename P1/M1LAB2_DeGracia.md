# The Sandwich Lab

## Steps to make a Pb&J Sandwich

## Assumptions:
- Two slices of bread are already plated
- Butter knife is already in hand
- peanut butter and grape jelly are already in sight

## Steps:
- Open the peanut butter jar
- Use a butter knife to spread the peanut butter on top of the first slice of bread
- Clean the butter knife
- Open the Grape Jelly jar
- Use the butter knife to spread the jelly on the second slice of bread 
- Combine the two slices together to were the jelly and the peanut butter sit in between the two slices

## Done When:
- Finished when first bread slice contains peanut butter spread on top and second slice contains grape jelly spread on top then stacked to were both spreads are facing eachother and lay in between the two slices of bread
